What we serve

Tea, scones,
and the day's
baking.

A short, considered menu. Nothing pretending to be more than it is — but everything made properly. Specific cakes change with what comes out of the oven.

Afternoon teas

Sweet or savoury — your call.

A tiered stand for the table. Bring an appetite; allow an hour or two.

  • Traditional sweet afternoon tea

    Finger sandwiches, warm scones with clotted cream and jam, a slice of the day's cake, and a bottomless pot of loose-leaf tea.

    Per person

  • Savoury afternoon tea

    Quiche, savoury scone, cheese and chutney, a small salad — for the people who'd rather not chase a sugar high. Same bottomless pot.

    Per person

  • Cream tea

    Two scones, clotted cream, jam, a pot of tea. The classic, kept short.

    Per person

  • Vegetarian & gluten-free

    Available for any of the above — please let us know when you order. Plant-based options are limited but real, not afterthoughts.

    On request
From the counter

Homemade cakes & scones.

Baked in the morning, set out on the counter when they've cooled. Available by the slice or to take away.

  • Victoria sponge

    Two layers of sponge, raspberry jam, a thick line of cream. Cut to order.

    By the slice

  • Lemon drizzle

    Tart, syrupy, made to be eaten the day it's baked.

    By the slice

  • Plain & fruit scones

    Warmed through. Cornish-style — cream first, then jam. Or the other way round, we won't argue.

    Each

  • Today's cake

    Coffee & walnut, carrot, a seasonal fruit cake — depending on the morning. Ask the counter.

    By the slice
Drinks

Loose-leaf tea, by the pot.

A short, deliberately small tea list. Coffee, properly made, for the rest.

  • Black teas

    English breakfast, Earl Grey, Assam — full-leaf, in the pot, with a strainer. As many top-ups as you want.

    By the pot

  • Green & herbal

    Green sencha, peppermint, chamomile, rooibos. Caffeine-free options for the late afternoon.

    By the pot

  • Coffee

    Americano, latte, flat white, cappuccino. Unhurried — pulled to order, not left to stew.

    Per cup

  • Cold drinks

    Cordials, juice, sparkling water. A small selection — we don't pretend to be a bar.

    Each
A note on pricing

Ask at the counter.

We've kept exact prices off the page so the menu can change a little with the season — the cake board updates almost daily, and tea prices shift with what we can source.

Stop in, look at the counter, ask the staff. Or call us beforehand on 01553 277546 if you'd like a steer for a group.
