Getting here

By foot, car or rail.

King's Lynn is well-connected. A short walk from town, parking on the doorstep, and a direct train to London.

  • On foot from the Tuesday Market Place

    Three minutes. Head south past the Custom House and along the river — Hanse House is on your right.

    3 min

  • Pay-and-display, on the Quay

    Spaces sit directly outside the building. South Quay car park is a few yards further along.

    Outside

  • From King's Lynn station

    Twelve minutes on foot, or a five-minute taxi. The 36 and X1 buses stop on Tower Street, two minutes from the Quay.

    12 min walk

  • From London King's Cross

    Direct to King's Lynn — under two hours, no changes. Worth the day trip.

    1h 45m
